# Overprint theatre / 套色剧场

Build the picture from a few translucent inks printed on paper. Different inks genuinely combine where they overlap; the overlap can carry a new state or relationship.

Separate the drawing into plate densities. Use solid masses and selected tints; filling every area with several halftones makes mud. Keep registration mostly stable within a shot. Controlled plate movement or an ink reveal can express an accent or a turning point; constant jitter is not a default texture.

The project's palette, subject, space and camera are free. A city can change when a new plate arrives; two fields can create a third; a printed object can become the next shot. These are possibilities, not a compulsory sequence. A documentary, playful story or abstract argument needs its own composition and pace.

Use silhouettes and a clear figure/ground relationship. A large environmental shape can establish scale; a closer reaction can make the same technique intimate. Avoid simply replacing every color in a fixed scene and calling it a new treatment.

Sound can follow the material—dry impacts, paper movement, restrained percussion—or the actual setting. If ink layers and instruments arrive together, that is one authored approach, not a global rule. Preserve room for speech.

Variation space: an architectural environment, a group interaction, a process, botanical forms, a geographic abstraction or a graphic argument. New characters and objects may be drawn directly; the code is not restricted to the example skyline or circles.

Implementation: `artwork` with `style: "print"`; plate-density `color`, `over`, and `art.printOptions`. Inspect highlights, skin, small halftones and moiré in the encoded output. [Guide](../illustration.md).
