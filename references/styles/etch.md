# Expressive ink / 叙事线刻

Use contour, black mass and selective hatching to make a thought or relationship visible. The material is an illustration, not a photograph with a monochrome filter.

Keep silhouettes readable and use line density to describe form, light and distance. A round surface takes curved marks; folds and hands need their own direction. Preserve paper as an active shape. One accent can carry attention or change, but its actual hue and meaning belong to the film.

Camera can move between an object detail and its relationship to a person or space. Shape transitions, drawn boundaries, paths becoming objects and changes of scale are available. Choose the action from the idea; the style does not require an eye, a giant hand, a small person or a fixed opening.

For characters, establish proportion and key poses, then interpolate the action. Keep gesture and expression legible at the final size. Separate important limbs and props; do not let a decorative stack cover the reaction the scene needs.

Sound may be dry, tactile or sparse: pencil movement, a paper shift, room tone, a small instrumental gesture. Use the materials and emotional change to choose it. No compulsory instrument, silence count or cut rate.

Variation space: a close study of an everyday object; two agents exchanging an idea; a spatial constraint; a page that becomes a landscape; a scientific observation. Draw new objects and arrangements with Canvas paths and stroke tools. The sample subjects are not the style's boundaries.

Implementation: `artwork` with `style: "etch"`; `hatch`, `path`, `camera`, `pose`, and the shape helpers. Inspect line weight, silhouette, negative space and the key change at both full and reduced size. [Guide](../illustration.md).
