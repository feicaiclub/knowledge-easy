# Material: select, draw, generate or combine

Begin with what the passage needs to show or make the viewer feel. Decide whether the image's job is evidence, observed action, an explanatory mechanism, a metaphor or atmosphere. An office, a clock or a thoughtful face is not automatically the best expression of work, time or reflection.

## Choose a route for the job

| Need | Search or create |
| --- | --- |
| Specific behavior, performance, light or camera language | Explore film/TV, documentary, creator-owned or stock material. Find a concrete shot and inspect its usable action, not only a keyword or thumbnail. |
| A real person, place, document or historical object | Trace an identifiable original source or institution. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia) and [The Met Open Access](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access) are starting points. Verify identity, context and reuse terms. |
| A visual metaphor, character or explanatory world | Draw original illustrations with the [code tools](illustration.md), design a project renderer, adapt appropriate artwork, or generate imagery when suitable tools are available. Choose the route that gives control over the actual idea. |
| Exact text, values, relationships or a mechanism | Use editable HTML/SVG/Canvas and deliberate movement. A diagram's relationships and changing states matter more than its label style. |
| A motion technique or composition reference | [Eyecannndy](https://eyecannndy.com/), artist portfolios and credited title/animation work can reveal how a technique operates. Study its construction; do not assume its complete composition or timing fits this film. |

These are starting points, not exclusive providers or evidence that a particular creator used them. A source is not a license for a new use; record the actual provenance and applicable terms. Generated/reconstructed visuals must not impersonate documentary evidence.

## Find stronger photographic candidates

Search in visual language as well as subject language: action, shot scale, angle, lighting direction, figure/ground relationship, mood and movement. [ShotDeck](https://shotdeck.com/) and [Flim](https://flim.ai/) can help locate cinematic references by visual characteristics. Identify the underlying work and seek an appropriate usable source; reference discovery and media acquisition are different steps.

Inspect the real shot across its usable range. Consider together:

- what the person or object actually does and how that changes the meaning;
- framing, gesture, expression, camera movement and a usable entrance/exit;
- motivated light, readable shadows/highlights, subject separation, texture and depth;
- continuity with neighboring shots, space for graphics, and whether the same source shot is already used.

Compare candidates on these properties when a passage is important or the first result is weak. A literal match with flat staging can be worse than a well-designed metaphor. An attractive unrelated shot can also weaken the explanation. Keep searching, redesign the passage visually, or draw it when neither criterion is met. Do not turn the provider with the easiest download into the film's creative boundary.

Grade the selected shots into the chosen visual language without erasing their original strengths. Darkening, desaturation and blur are tools, not the definition of cinematic quality. A bright scene may be the stronger dramatic choice.

## Original illustration is a production route

First design the image: subject, gesture, spatial relationship, silhouette, hierarchy, palette logic, material and the change the audience sees. Code then draws and animates it. SVG/Canvas are not limited to charts or icons; reusable brush, shape and pose tools support new characters, objects and environments.

A style profile preserves how marks and materials behave. It must not fix what every future film depicts. Different topics may require different subjects, composition and animation even within one drawing language. Keep precise labels editable and compose them with the artwork rather than using them to compensate for an image that communicates nothing.

For mixed media, deliberately align edge treatment, scale, light, perspective and color relationships across photographs, cutouts, drawings and texture. A cutout can act, an archival detail can become a diagram, or an object can bridge two shots. Imported art and animations still need editable timing and deterministic rendering. Rebuild or use another route when an asset cannot be adapted to the argument.

## Provenance and reusable resources

Use project records for sources, used ranges, identity, context and rights. Do not infer a creator's download site from the appearance of a film. Distinguish confirmed original works, possible influences and unknown origins.

The package supplies language fonts, public-domain map geometry, the default independent voice reference, classic layout previews and code drawing tools. Raw third-party films, stock clips and model weights are not bundled. [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) identify the licenses; [demo media](../assets/packaging/demo-media/README.md) describes the legacy catalog's sample slots. Those sample assets are not a shot list for a new film.
